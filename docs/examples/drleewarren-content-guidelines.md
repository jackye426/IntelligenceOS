# Content guidelines, derived from @drleewarren

**Source:** 62 of 262 posts read in full (caption, transcript, OCR frames) across a catalogue spanning Feb 2022 to Sep 2026. Quantitative tests run on the 43 recent-era posts that have timestamped transcripts.

**Status of the evidence.** Everything marked *measured* comes from counts across that sample. Everything marked *inferred* is a reading of content structure that the data is consistent with but does not prove. We have no retention data, no follower history and no traffic-source split for a peer account, so the chain from "opening structure" to "watch time" to "reach" is assumed, not observed. Treat this as a strong prior to test against our own Studio numbers, not as settled law.

**Talent-agnostic.** Written so it applies to any presenter and any clinical topic. Where an example is needed, `[PROBLEM]` and `[MECHANISM]` are placeholders.

---

## 0. The one-line thesis

His account went from a 3.5-year ceiling of 7,295 views to a 16.6M post. The change was not production value, not posting time, not hashtags. It was **who the content is about.**

Before: the presenter is the subject. Dad jokes, vlogs, career nostalgia, podcast clips.
After: the viewer's problem is the subject, and the credential is the instrument used on it.

Every rule below is downstream of that.

---

## 1. The first 15 seconds

*Measured*, comparing his top performers (2x+ their rolling local baseline, n=19) against his underperformers (below 1x, n=16):

| Metric in first 15s | Top | Bottom |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract category words per 100 words | **0.2** | **1.28** |
| Total words spoken | 50 | 60 |
| Words per second | 3.53 | 4.0 |
| Sentences | 3 | 3 |
| Words per sentence | **15** | **20** |
| Seconds to first "you/your" | 0.0 | 1.69 |

### Rules

1. **Ban abstract category words.** This is the single strongest discriminator in the whole dataset. Losers use 6.4x more of them.
   Banned: *journey, wellness, empowerment, healing, hope, transformation, higher performance, flourish, potential, support, advocacy*
   Wanted: the specific thing that happened in their week.

2. **Say less, slower, in shorter sentences.** Roughly 50 words across 15 seconds, three sentences, about 15 words each. Slower than instinct says. Do not cram.

3. **"You" or "your" in the first sentence.** Top performers hit it at second zero.

4. **Do not pay off the pain before ~17 seconds.** On his best-structured posts the relief line lands at 17 to 20 seconds. The first 15 builds the problem; the release comes just after.

### What does NOT discriminate

*Measured, and worth knowing because it contradicts common advice:*

- **Stating the credential in the opening.** 8/20 of his top posts do it, 9/16 of his bottom posts do it. Neutral.
- **An explicit time contract** ("give me two minutes"). 4/20 top, 3/16 bottom. Neutral.

His single worst-performing recent post opens *"So I'm a brain surgeon and I'm going to show you how to operate your brain the way it was designed for your healing and hope and higher performance"* and did **0.08x**. Credential present, promise present, still dead, because every noun after the credential is abstract.

**The credential is permission to speak. It is not a hook. What matters is what it is pointed at.**

### The three opening archetypes that work

**A. Imperative.** Under 10 words, a command.
> "Stop your negative self-talk." (2.8s, then mechanism, then "So knock it off" at 17s)

**B. Promise, then cost, then vernacular restatement.**
> 0-5s promise → 5-11s "but we'll have to nerd out for a couple of minutes" → 12-16s benefit restated in plainer words → named mechanism at 16s

**C. Symptom scene.** Credential plus the named symptom, then their Tuesday in sensory detail.
> 0-7s credential + `[PROBLEM]` → 7-17s "You're staring at your computer screen. You read the same sentence four times. You walk into a room, you can't remember why" → 17-20s the shameful inner line: "Something's wrong with me."

## 2. Full video structure

Six beats, present in every top performer.

1. **Credential plus second-person promise**, inside 3 seconds.
2. **Symptom scene before diagnosis.** Their experience in sensory detail, ending on the self-critical line they actually say in their head.
3. **One named mechanism with a proper noun.** He uses Hebb's Law, ergodic systems, affect labeling, the reticular activating system, metacognition. Never "studies show." Give the viewer a term they can repeat to a professional.
4. **Second authority arriving as convergence, not concession.** For him that is scripture, framed as "and scripture said it first." The portable principle is: two independent frames agreeing, neither subordinated.
5. **The reframe that removes shame.** This is the share trigger. "The ache that stays doesn't mean you're stuck. It means you loved well enough that your brain refused to let go."
6. **Save and share, both with a named target.** Never "share this." Always "save this for the next time `[SITUATION]`, share it with someone who `[CONDITION]`."

**Time contract deliberately broken.** He says "give me two minutes" over a 177-second video. Cheap entry price, over-delivery. *Inferred* that this helps; not directly measured.

## 3. Production spec

*Measured from OCR frame sampling at 0.0s, 0.5s, 1.0s and 2.0s.*

**Static title banner is the clearest production tell.** On his winners, all four sampled frames return the identical full sentence. The title is a fixed overlay. On his losers, the frames return fragments (`""` → `"EVER DONE"` → `"SOMETHING"` → `"REGRETTED?"`), meaning word-by-word animated auto-captions and no title.

> **Rule: a viewer landing at any point in the first two seconds must see the entire promise, not one word of it.** Burn a static title card overlay. Do not rely on animated captions to carry the hook.

*(Caveat: OCR samples only 0 to 2s, so this confirms the banner persists through the opening, not the whole video.)*

**Setting as authenticity marker.** His frames repeatedly leak an EXIT sign, `Great Plains Health` signage and `FIGS` scrubs. He films in the workplace, in uniform, apparently mid-shift. The environment does credentialing work that the script does not have to.

**Persistent lower-third** carrying name and specialty.

---

## 4. Length

*Measured.* Top performers span 40 to 177 seconds with no optimum inside that band: 54s, 100s, 177s, 48s, 45s, 171s all outperformed.

- **Floor:** below 45s works only if the caption carries the argument (see §5).
- **Ceiling:** a 561-second post did 0.198x. Above roughly 180 seconds it stops working.
- **Target: 60 to 150 seconds.**

Length is not the lever. Structure is. Do not shorten a good structure to chase a duration target.

## 5. Caption spec

The most mechanically copyable element, and the one we are most likely to under-build.

*Measured:* caption length 1,500 to 2,000 characters, at a **caption-to-transcript character ratio of 0.8 to 1.1**. The caption is a near-verbatim mirror of the spoken script, not a summary.

### Structure

1. Cold-open thesis line, usually the spoken hook verbatim
2. Mechanism paragraph containing the one named concept
3. The second authority, with a citation
4. The reframe
5. **CTA stack in fixed order:** Follow → Save (named future moment) → Share (named person) → Link in bio

### Rules

- **Every post ships twice in one upload:** once as speech, once as readable text. This is why his save rates run 40 to 70 per 1k. The post has to work muted and has to work as a saved artifact.
- **Disclaimers go at the END of the caption, never the top.** His clinical-adjacent winner buries "check with your doctor" near the close. His underperforming series opens with a bracketed disclaimer. Same legal protection, very different cost.
- **Hashtags are not load-bearing.** *Measured:* three posts in the sample carried zero hashtags and did 156K, 171K and 195K. When he uses them it is four, a fixed core set plus one or two topical. Do not spend effort here.

### The highest-value single finding

His caption template existed a **full year** before the matching video format. An Aug 2025 post carries the exact structure (`Neuroscience Nugget:` → `Faith Fact:` → reframe → arrow chain) in the caption, attached to a comedy-bit video. 923 views. The caption was teaching and the video was doing stand-up.

**Use captions as the cheap idea testbed.** Ship the thesis as a caption on low-cost footage. If it beats its local baseline, produce it properly. Do not let the caption run ahead of the video for a year the way he did.

## 6. Hook naming formulas

Four repeatable title patterns, all prefixed with the credential:

| Formula | Pattern | His example |
|---|---|---|
| How-to on a felt symptom | `[CREDENTIAL]: How to [relieve PROBLEM]` | "How to clear Brain Fog" |
| **Myth correction** | `[CREDENTIAL]: [COMMON BELIEF] is not [WHAT THEY THINK]` | "Depression is not a Faith Problem" |
| Contrarian | `[PROBLEM]? The problem is NOT [OBVIOUS CULPRIT]` | "Anxious? The problem is NOT your brain" |
| Imperative | `[CREDENTIAL]: [command]` | "It's time to lock in!" |

**Myth correction is the sharpest lever.** It hijacks a term people already search and argue about. "Does your body really keep the score?" borrows a bestselling book title and then corrects it. We have obvious equivalents in any clinical space where public belief and clinical reality diverge.

## 7. Pick the metric before you pick the format

*Measured.* Content type and response type are tightly coupled, and inversely:

| Content type | Comments per 1k | Saves per 1k | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal story / grief | 4 to 17 | 12 to 20 | Community, replies, depth |
| Teaching / mechanism | 1.2 to 3 | 40 to 70 | Distribution and reach |

His 16.6M post has the **lowest** comment rate in the sample (1.3 per 1k) against 41.2 shares per 1k. It was passed along, not discussed.

**Decide which instrument you are using before you write.** They are not interchangeable and you cannot optimise both in one post.

## 8. Cadence and the compounding rule

This is where he lost the most value, and it is the cheapest lesson to take for free.

*Measured.* He received a strong signal three times:

- **Oct 2025:** rolling baseline tripled (650 → 1,620) on 22 posts in a month. He dropped to **one post in November**. Growth stalled for eight months.
- **8 Feb 2026:** one post did 53,300 views at **22.9x** local baseline. Next post five days later did 2,821. Then an **82-day gap**.
- **3 Aug 2026:** viral post. He posted **8 to 10 times a week for the next month**. Baseline went from ~29,000 to ~149,000.

Same input, opposite response, roughly two orders of magnitude difference in outcome.

> **Rule: when a post beats its local baseline by 10x or more, clear the calendar that week.** Ship variations on that exact angle daily while the signal is live. The window is days, not months.

**Sustained cadence target during a live signal: 7 to 10 posts per week.** Baseline cadence: daily.

## 9. Anti-patterns, with evidence

All ratios are against his own rolling local baseline at the time.

| Anti-pattern | His example | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| **Post about your own news** | "BIG NEWS!" announcing his book hit the NYT list. Transcript: "Look at this. Look, hey! Look at this." | **0.063x** |
| Series requiring prior episodes | "This is part three, make sure you go back and watch the other parts" | 0.081x |
| In-group jargon in sentence one | "Self-brain surgeons relentlessly refuse to participate in their own demise" | 0.164x |
| Benefit aimed at a third party | Back-to-school advice for parents (helps your kids, not you) | 0.173x |
| Off-platform CTA | "Go to my YouTube channel for the full video" | 0.164x |
| Product/app announcement | "So excited the coaching app is available now" | 0.202x |
| Pure infomercial | Dec 2025 launch-team ad, says the URL five times in 99 seconds | Killed 8 months of momentum |
| Borrowed voice | Feb 2026 post using generic manifestation audio, contradicting his own stated position | Well below ceiling |

**The worst post of his entire peak period is the one celebrating his own success, published during his best week.** 8,163 views against a baseline of 130,450. A 94% penalty.

### The series trap

*Measured, and worth naming separately.* His four-part series has his **highest save rates in the whole sample** (45.2, 42.8, 37.5, 35.9 per 1k) and his **lowest reach ratios** (0.63, 0.88, 0.08, 0.27).

People who reach it love it. The opening gate stops most of them arriving. If we make series content, every episode must stand alone and must never open by asking for prior commitment.

## 10. What NOT to copy

- **His second authority is scripture.** It works because his audience is pre-selected for it and he holds genuine standing in both frames. Copying the *move* (two independent frames converging) is right. Copying the *content* is not.
- **The surgical metaphor as a verb system** (scrub in, take a biopsy, malpractice against yourself, prescription). It works because he operates on the organ he discusses. Most credentials do not supply a metaphor engine this cleanly. Do not force one.
- **Personal tragedy at that magnitude.** Not available and not to be manufactured.
- **Recycling winners within two weeks.** *Observed:* his 31 Aug post reuses the 16 Aug caption verbatim and performed better (2.44x vs 1.31x). Probably real, but n=1. Test before adopting.

## 11. Pre-publish checklist

```
FIRST 15 SECONDS
[ ] Zero abstract category words (journey, wellness, healing, hope,
    empowerment, transformation, potential, support)
[ ] "You" or "your" in the first sentence
[ ] ~50 words, 3 sentences, ~15 words each
[ ] A specific named symptom, not a topic area
[ ] Relief line does NOT appear before ~17s

STRUCTURE
[ ] Symptom scene precedes mechanism
[ ] Exactly one named mechanism with a proper noun
[ ] Reframe that removes shame, stated as one quotable sentence
[ ] Save + share instruction, both with named targets

PRODUCTION
[ ] Static title banner, full sentence, legible from frame 1
[ ] Persistent lower-third with name and credential
[ ] 60 to 150 seconds

CAPTION
[ ] 1,500 to 2,000 characters
[ ] Mirrors the spoken script (0.8 to 1.1 char ratio), not a summary
[ ] CTA stack: Follow → Save → Share → Link
[ ] Disclaimer at the END, not the top

FAILS AUTOMATICALLY IF
[ ] It is about us, our news, our product, or our milestone
[ ] It requires a previous episode
[ ] It opens on our internal vocabulary
[ ] The benefit lands on someone other than the viewer
[ ] It sends people off-platform
```

## 12. How to measure ourselves

- **Judge posts against a rolling local baseline**, not raw views and not the all-time catalogue median. A post is compared to its publish-time neighbours. Old posts otherwise look inflated purely for having been live longer.
- **Views are cross-sectional.** They measure how a post landed on the audience that already existed, not how the audience grew. A rising view floor is a proxy for growth, never proof.
- **Saves per 1k is the earliest honest quality signal.** His save rate told the truth months before his view count did: a May 2026 post got 6,362 views at 59.3 saves per 1k, the highest save rate in the entire 262-post catalogue. The format was already right; only the distribution was missing.
- **Do not average across accounts or eras.** Mixed cohorts produce meaningless medians.

### Open question to close with our own data

We cannot see his retention. Average watch time, 3-second hold and finish rate are owner-only. Once we have shipped 5 to 10 posts built to this spec, pull our own Studio retention curves and check whether the 17-second relief point actually maps to a retention inflection. That closes the one causal gap in this document.
